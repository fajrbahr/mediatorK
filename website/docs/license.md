## On knowledge and attribution

I believe science is a cumulative thing. It cannot and should not be owned — it belongs to communities, to society, to everyone.

This library could not exist without:

- **Allah** — first and above all.
- **JetBrains** — for Kotlin.
- **Jimmy Bogard** — for [MediatR](https://github.com/jbogard/MediatR), the direct inspiration for this library.
- **MediatR** — without it, this project would have no direction.
- **DDD (Domain-Driven Design)** — which gave MediatR its purpose.
- **Design Patterns** — which gave DDD its vocabulary.

Every book depends on another book. Every idea completes a prior idea. No one creates from nothing — and anyone who claims otherwise is selling something.

The only true zero-prior-work invention would be a new mathematics, a new language with no roots in any human tongue, a field of science with zero prior human interest. Everything else is built on what came before.

Knowledge is a chain. Claiming a link in it as exclusively yours is the real fiction.
